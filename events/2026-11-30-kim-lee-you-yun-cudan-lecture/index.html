Speakers
Jin Kim, Byunghwee Lee, Taekho You, and Jinhyuk Yun
Soongsil University (Seoul), Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (Daejeon), University of Virginia (Charlottesville), Indiana University (Bloomington), and Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (Bochum).

Lecture title
Art Through the Lens of Data Science: From Big Data to Human-AI Mixed Society.

Abstract:
How does artistic creativity evolve over centuries, and what happens when artificial intelligence becomes an active participant in cultural production? In this talk, we explore the quantitative evolution of art through the lens of data science and multimodal AI. Moving beyond traditional art-historical narratives, we introduce our recent PNAS study, which leverages context-aware multimodal AI to analyze large-scale visual and historical records across five centuries. By capturing both stylistic features and historical context, our approach uncovers hidden evolutionary pathways and structural transitions in art history that conventional analyses often overlook. Finally, we look ahead to the emerging era of human-AI mixed societies, discussing how generative AI is reshaping collective creativity, cultural ecosystems, and the future trajectory of human knowledge.

Jinhyuk Yun will be present in person and introduce of the research team. Byungwhee Lee will present the background and preceding research trajectory, walking through earlier studies of the team and the broader context leading up to this work. Taekho You will present core methodology and main findings of their recent PNAS paper. Jinhyuk Yun will wrap up the talk by sharing some personal closing perspectives, a future outlook, and open thoughts to kick off our discussion.

Short Bios:
Jin Kim is a researcher in the AX Group at Tantancore Inc. She received her M.S. from the Advanced Data Science Lab, Department of Intelligent Semiconductors, Soongsil University, and her B.S. in Global Media from Soongsil University. Her research uses multimodal generative AI to study how art and visual culture evolve alongside society.

Byunghwee Lee is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Virginia School of Data Science, working with Prof. Yong-Yeol Ahn. He received his Ph.D. in Physics from KAIST. He aims to develop interpretable frameworks to uncover the principles underlying the structure and evolution of human beliefs, behavior, and creativity. His research combines statistical physics, network science, and machine learning to analyze large-scale behavioral and cultural data.

Taekho You is a Research Assistant Professor at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology. His research focuses on revealing macroscopic academic dynamics through microscopic patterns with large-scale data and network science. He is also interested in how AI embeds hidden knowledge and how we can find this knowledge.

Jinhyuk Yun is an Associate Professor at Soongsil University and a Visiting Researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy. Viewing knowledge as a living network, he integrates statistical physics and AI to decode how science, culture, and human-AI ecosystems evolve.
