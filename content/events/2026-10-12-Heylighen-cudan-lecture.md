***Lecture title***  
**Stigmergy and collective intelligence: coordination without control**  

***Abstract***
Can a group think together without a leader or a plan? This presentation  introduces stigmergy: coordination through traces left in a shared medium that prompt others to act. Examples from social insects, Wikipedia, and science show how contributions can accumulate, be corrected, and guide further work. The Noosphere Lab experiment has put this idea to a real time test. Participants anonymously edited a shared story and a discussion paper, using text and “meta-marks” to suggest what needs attention. The experiments ask whether such interaction can produce coherent results while preserving diverse perspectives and reducing the pressures that lead to groupthink.
