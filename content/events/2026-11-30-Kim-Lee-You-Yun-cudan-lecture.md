***Speakers***  
**Jin Kim, Byunghwee Lee, Taekho You, and Jinhyuk Yun**  
Soongsil University (Seoul), Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (Daejeon), University of Virginia (Charlottesville),  Indiana University (Bloomington), and Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (Bochum).  

***Lecture title***  
**Art Through the Lens of Data Science: From Big Data to Human-AI Mixed Society.**    

***Presentation structure:***
Prof. Jinhyuk Yun will be present in person and introduce of the research team. Dr. Byungwhee Lee will present the background and preceding research trajectory, walking through earlier studies of the team and the broader context leading up to this work. Prof. Taekho You will present core methodology and main findings of their recent [PNAS paper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2517969123). Prof. Jinhyuk Yun will wrap up the talk by sharing some personal closing perspectives, a future outlook, and open thoughts to kick off our discussion.
